# Variable roles and units

The report discusses house prices, job population, population density, and new housing. Its wording about dependent variables needs clarification before constructing any model.

| Candidate field | Definition needed before reuse |
|---|---|
| House price | Statistic, currency, property coverage, and observation year |
| Job population | Workplace or resident measure, population base, and period |
| Population density | Population denominator and area units |
| New houses | Completions, stock, or another measure; period and geographic unit |

A later model should explicitly name its outcome and predictors and explain missing-value handling. Do not interpret a correlation as a causal effect or treat differently dated variables as contemporaneous without justification.
