# Study questions and scope

The aims on PDF page 3 are to describe the spatial distribution of London house prices, consider patterns suggestive of spatial autocorrelation, and discuss potential explanations. The available evidence consists of a final report with descriptive statistics and maps.

Keep three tasks distinct: describing variation, testing a spatial relationship, and explaining a mechanism. The report's visual patterns motivate questions; the supplied material does not establish that a regression or formal spatial-autocorrelation test was fitted. A future test needs the source observations, geographic units, and a justified neighbourhood definition.

This portfolio is an academic archive, not a current house-price estimate or an investment recommendation.
